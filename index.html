🇹🇷 Oyun Hakkında

Patlat!, dikey ekran formatında tek elle akıcı şekilde oynanabilen, zengin taktiksel derinliğe sahip modern bir 2D balon kule savunma oyunudur. Çayır, Kumsal, Karlı Vadi ve Volkan haritalarında sevimli hayvan kulelerini yerleştir, 30 tur boyunca güçlenen renkli balon dalgalarına karşı savunmanı kur ve dev Zeplin Boss'unu patlat!

🇬🇧 About the Game

Patlat! is a vibrant, portrait-mode 2D balloon tower defense game designed for intuitive one-handed play. Strategically deploy clever animal towers across Meadow, Beach, Snowy Valley, and Volcano maps. Survive 30 rounds of relentless balloon waves and defeat the giant Zeppelin Boss!

🦔 5 Clever Animal Towers Hedgehog, Cactus, Beaver, Penguin, and Owl.
🗺️ 4 Distinct Maps Meadow, Beach, Snowy Valley, and Volcano.
👑 Global Leaderboards Apple Game Center & Google Play Games Services.
✈️ Fully Playable Offline Enjoy full defense action anywhere, anytime.
🔒 Privacy-First Design No account required, no personal data harvested.
🎬 Purely Opt-In Rewarded Ads No interstitial or forced ads; watch only for rewards.
